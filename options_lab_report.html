Assumptions

  • Entry at the first 1-min bar at/after 09:20, at that bar's close; strikes are the nearest listed to the target distance.
  • Stop-loss fills exactly at the trigger price + 0 pts slippage; the other leg is bought back at its price that minute (+ the same slippage).
  • Legs held to the close settle at intrinsic value vs the index's last 1-min close.
  • ₹ figures use 420 qty and ₹80 charges per trade (another ₹80 when stopped out).
  • Days where either leg's entry premium was below 0.3 pts are skipped.
  • Sparse option bars are forward-filled; a stop-loss triggers when a 1-min high reaches it.
