Volume Breakout strategy: F&O large caps, 2015–2026

A breakout on its own did no better than buying a random stock. It worked when volume confirmed it: heavy volume on the breakout day, and volume running high in the two weeks before and the two weeks after. With that, 70% of trades made money in both the 2015–21 data and the unseen 2022–26 check. Multi-year breakouts beat multi-month ones, and results were weak during market-wide corrections, crises and wars.

Universe: 116 F&O-traded Nifty 100 stocks with 1-minute data (the other ~100 F&O stocks are not in this dataset). Daily bars built from 1-minute candles, Feb 2015 – 8 Apr 2026. Entry at the next session's open, 0.25% round-trip cost. "Random entry" is the average 3-month return of any stock bought on any day after the same cost. Not SEBI-registered advice.

The final rule (Volume Breakout)
  1. Breakout: the stock closes above its highest price of the last 6 months, 1 year or 2+ years, and that old high was set at least 20 sessions earlier, so it is a real base, not a stock already making new highs every day.
  2. Heavy volume on the day: breakout-day volume is at least 2× its 50-day average.
  3. Two weeks before: average volume over the 10 sessions up to and including the breakout is at least 1.5× the normal level (the 50 sessions before that).
  4. Two weeks after: wait 10 sessions. Enter at the next open only if the close is still above the breakout level and volume over those 10 sessions averaged at least 1.3× normal.
  5. Exit: sell after 63 sessions (about 3 months). Holding 6 months did better still (see table).

Read before trading

Only today's Nifty 100 names are in the data, so stocks whose breakouts failed and later left the index are missing. That flatters every breakout result. Stops cut the winners (see exits), and results were poor in corrections, crises and wars.

4. When it works

Breakouts worked in normal markets and best right after crashes (the 2020–21 recovery). They failed during corrections (FPI selling 2021–22 and 2024–25, the 2022 rate hikes), crises and war periods. For the simpler breakout + volume version, requiring NIFTY above its averages did not help, because some of the best breakouts came while the index was still weak. For the final rule, trades taken in a healthy market did better than the 20 taken while NIFTY was weak. The losing years for the final rule were 2018 and 2025.

Method and caveats
  • No look-ahead. The breakout and its volume are known at the signal day's close; entry is the next open. In the confirmation versions, the two weeks after the breakout are complete before the entry on day 11.
  • One trade per stock at a time, and a stock cannot signal again within 63 sessions of its last signal.
  • Normal volume is the 50-session average ending two weeks before the signal, so the two-week window doesn't inflate its own baseline.
  • Survivor bias. Only stocks in today's Nifty 100 are included. Breakouts in stocks that later collapsed and left the index (or never joined it) are missing. Expect real results to be lower.
  • Chosen after seeing the data. The 1.5× and 1.3× thresholds and the 63-session hold were picked from a small grid on 2015–21; 2022–26 is the honest check. The threshold table in section 2 shows nearby settings gave similar results.
  • Universe. 116 of roughly 220 F&O stocks. To run all F&O stocks, daily data for the rest can be pulled with Kite Connect and fed to research/breakout_lab.py.
  • Not tested: taxes, position sizing, and anything after 8 Apr 2026.
